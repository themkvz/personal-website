Running a product across desktop, mobile, and a web-based administration
panel means the same domain logic has to behave identically in three very
different runtimes.

## Shared foundations

The workflows that actually stayed maintainable were the ones where the
domain logic lived in one place and each platform's UI layer was a thin
adapter over it, not a reimplementation.

## Open threads

- How much of the validation logic can move into a shared package versus
  staying duplicated for platform-specific UX reasons.
- Where the administration interface's data-fetching patterns diverge from
  the consumer app's, and whether that divergence is justified.
