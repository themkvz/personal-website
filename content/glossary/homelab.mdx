---
term: Homelab
summary: A personal setup of self-hosted servers and services, usually running at home, used to run software a hobbyist wants direct control over.
related:
  - self-hosting
---

A homelab is the infrastructure side of self-hosting: a spare machine, a small server, or a rack of them, running services like media servers, home automation, backups, or development environments outside any cloud provider. It sits at the intersection of a hobby and genuine infrastructure practice. The same skills (networking, containers, monitoring, backup strategy) that keep a homelab running also keep production systems running, just with lower stakes when something breaks.

The appeal for a software engineer is often less about saving money over cloud services and more about having a real system to break and fix, with full visibility into a stack that a managed platform would otherwise hide.
