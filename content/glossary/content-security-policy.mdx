---
term: Content Security Policy
summary: An HTTP header naming the sources a page may load scripts and other content from, as a defense against injection attacks.
aka:
  - CSP
---

A Content Security Policy is a declarative allowlist, delivered as a response header, naming the origins a page may load scripts, styles, images, fonts, and other resources from. Its main job is limiting the damage of a cross-site scripting vulnerability: even if an attacker injects a script tag, the browser blocks scripts that the policy does not allow.

A report-only mode and a `report-uri` or `report-to` directive let a policy be rolled out and monitored. The browser sends a report of what it would have blocked before the policy is switched to enforcement. This is how a policy gets tightened without breaking the page it protects on the first attempt.
